Altare is an indie mobile game studio passionate about creating engaging, fun, and visually stunning games. We believe in the power of mobile gaming to bring joy to millions of players worldwide.

From hyper-casual to mid-core, we explore different genres and push creative boundaries to deliver unique gaming experiences that players love. Our latest flagship title ChopHero: Relic Quest is a one-thumb action RPG set in a cursed desert world — fully offline, built around auto-combat and deep weapon progression.
